About Japanese Reading Aide

Helping Japanese learners read with fewer interruptions and build vocabulary with confidence.

✿ Our Mission

Japanese uses multiple writing systems and does not usually separate words with spaces. We help learners find vocabulary and turn it into study material.

Key Features

Paste or upload Japanese text, inspect vocabulary, save readings, generate flashcards, and track vocabulary coverage.

Who Is This For?

Japanese language learners, including students studying Japanese at Hunter College.

About This Project

Created as a student web application project. This demonstration stores readings and progress locally in your browser.
