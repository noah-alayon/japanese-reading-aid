About Japanese Reading Aide

Helping Japanese learners read with fewer interruptions and build vocabulary with confidence.

✿ Our Mission

Japanese Reading Aide is a study companion designed to heip Japanese learners read more, understand more, and grow their vocabulary. By analyzing texts and highlighting unfamiliar words, we make it easier to build reading skills and find material that matches your level.

Key Features

  • Paste or upload Japanese text.
  • Inspect Japanese vocabulary and English definitions.
  • Save readings for later review.
  • Generate flashcards to study vocabulary.
  • Track vocabulary coverage and learning progress.

Who Is This For?

This tool is designed for Japanese learners, including students in Hunter College's Japanese courses, self-learners, and anyone who wants to improve their reading skills.

About This Project

Japanese Reading Aide is a college project created by Anthony Etwaru, Noah Alayon, and Salma Garden Mendez to make Japanese reading practice more accessible and effective for learners.
