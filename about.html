About Japanese Reading Aide

Helping Japanese learners improve their reading comprehension and expand their vocabulary.

Our Mission

Japanese Reading Aide helps learners read Japanese texts with greater confidence while building their vocabulary.

Key Features

  • Analyze Japanese text from files or pasted text
  • Identify unfamiliar Japanese vocabulary
  • View English vocabulary definitions
  • Save vocabulary for flashcard practice

Who Is This For?

Japanese Reading Aide is designed for students taking Japanese courses, self-learners, and anyone interested in improving their Japanese reading skills.

About This Project

Japanese Reading Aide is a college web development project designed to help Japanese learners understand unfamiliar vocabulary while reading.
